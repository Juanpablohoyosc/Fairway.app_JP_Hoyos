Razorcalc

A full graphing and finance calculator for student-athletes in Fayetteville. Plan a budget, compare NIL offers, check a car payment, and get through stats and calculus homework on the bus to the next game.

Start with an example

Each example loads the numbers and puts the cursor where you need it.

Money moves: TVM Solver

Press APPS, 1 for Finance, then 1 for TVM Solver. Type a value in each field and press ENTER to move down. Put the cursor on the unknown and press ALPHA ENTER to solve it.

Cash you receive is positive, cash you pay is negative. A loan has a positive PV and a negative PMT. Setting P/Y also sets C/Y. The Finance menu also has npv(, irr(, bal(, ΣPrn(, ΣInt(, ►Eff( and dbd(.

Graphing for class

Press Y= and type a function using X,T,θ,n, then GRAPH. TRACE walks along the curve, ZOOM changes the view, and WINDOW sets the range. 2nd TRACE finds zeros, minimums, maximums and intersections, and 2nd GRAPH shows a table.

Stats for the season

STAT 1 opens the list editor. Enter your sprint times, lifts or shooting percentages in L₁ and L₂. Then STAT ▶ CALC gives you 1-Var Stats, 2-Var Stats and regressions. 2nd Y= turns on a scatter plot, and 2nd VARS has the normal, binomial and Poisson distributions.

Everything else

MATH, 2nd x⁻¹ matrices, 2nd STAT list tools, 2nd APPS angles, PRGM short programs, 2nd PRGM drawing, 2nd STO→ recall, and MODE for decimals, notation and degrees. 2nd 0 opens the catalog of every function.

Your keyboard works too: numbers and operators type directly, Enter evaluates, Backspace deletes, Esc clears, and letters type variables. Memory stays in this browser between visits.
